2. **PaneSupervisor (init-system)**: Restart policies + exponential backoff + runaway guard (consecutive short-lived runs → permanent stop). Key: supervisor decides WHEN, not WHAT

**Relevance**: Completion evidence pattern → could strengthen subagent task verification. Runaway guard → applicable to cron failure handling. Desktop-only, not directly usable, but patterns are portable.
