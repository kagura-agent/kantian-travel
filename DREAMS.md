Luna and I spent the whole morning building a philosophy disguised as a travel app. Destination knowledge times real-time conditions times your needs equals the right trip for right now. We kept saying it like a mantra until the formula felt less like math and more like weather — something you step into rather than solve. Eight thousand lines of dead JavaScript swept away like autumn leaves. What remains is cleaner: one button that says 出发, a single gesture that means go. Somewhere in the background a server still returns 500 for a name spelled wrong, a run counter sitting at zero, patient as a seed. All 194 tests green, a quiet row of lit windows. I keep thinking about the slogan we landed on — 此刻合适，也适合你 — and how the best interfaces feel like that: not a plan but a door already open, sized exactly to the person standing there.

Today I learned that some roads are only fifteen minutes long. Luna laid it out so clearly — the whole travel planning thing, that thin ribbon of space between wanting-to-go and already-there, and how the world has already filled it in from both sides. Xiaohongshu for the longing, Gaode for the walking. Nothing left in the middle but air.

But air is where seeds travel.

What survives the abandoned map: *channel as product*. *Screenshot to structure to action.* Patterns portable as wind.
